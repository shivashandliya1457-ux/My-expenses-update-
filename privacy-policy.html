1. Overview

My Expenses Report is a personal expense management and bookkeeping utility application designed to help users track their personal expenses, budget limits, and debt ledger (Udhar Khata). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information.

2. Information We Collect

  • Expense and Financial Records: Categories, amounts, notes, payment modes (Cash/UPI/Card), and debt records entered manually by you.
  • Authentication & Account Information:
    • Username and encrypted password created by the user for local security isolation.
    • Google Account (Gmail): Verified securely via Google Credential Manager & Google Firebase Authentication for authentic 1-click identity verification, account recovery, and Google Cloud Firestore data synchronization.
  • Biometric Data: If enabled, device fingerprint or screen lock verification is handled entirely by the local Android OS BiometricPrompt API. We do not collect, transmit, or store your biometric data.

3. Google Account (Gmail) Verification Policy (Strictly for User Verification Only)

🔒 Zero Spam & Zero Marketing Guarantee / केवल असली यूजर वेरिफिकेशन के लिए:

We strictly collect and use the user's verified Google (Gmail) account ONLY for authentic identity verification, account recovery, fraud prevention, and encrypted cloud synchronization via Google Cloud Firestore.

  • Exclusive Purpose: Your Gmail identity is verified cryptographically via Google Identity Services (Jetpack Credential Manager) and Google Firebase to confirm user identity and protect expense books from unauthorized access.
  • No Secondary Use: We NEVER use your email address for marketing emails, newsletter spam, promotional campaigns, advertising, or cross-app profiling.
  • No Third-Party Sharing: Your email address is never sold, leased, or shared with data brokers, marketing agencies, or advertisers.
  • Zero Sensitive Permissions: The app does NOT request access to your Gmail inbox, emails, or Google Drive files. Only your public Google profile identity (email and name) is used for authentic user verification.

4. What We DO NOT Collect (Zero Sensitive Permissions)

We believe in least-privilege security. Our application:

  • DOES NOT read, send, or access your personal text messages (SMS). OTPs are sent via Google Firebase cloud infrastructure.
  • DOES NOT access your contact book or address list.
  • DOES NOT track your real-time GPS location.
  • DOES NOT access your device camera or microphone.
  • DOES NOT sell, rent, or trade user data to third-party advertisers or brokers.

5. Cloud Data Storage & Security

Cloud backup and synchronization are powered by Google Cloud Firestore and Firebase Authentication under strict Zero-Trust security rules. All communications use HTTPS/TLS encryption.

6. Data Retention & Account Deletion

You have full ownership of your data:

  • Local Purge: You can delete any individual transaction or empty trash at any time.
  • Full Account Deletion: You can permanently purge your entire account, all expenses, and all cloud backups instantly via Settings > Delete Account inside the app.

7. Children's Privacy

Our app does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children.
